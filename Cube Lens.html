How it works inside

  • Core: a 3‑axis cross that holds everything together.
  • 6 centers: one sticker each, screwed to the core. They never move relative to each other, so they define each face’s color.
  • 12 edges: two stickers, slot between centers and corners.
  • 8 corners: three stickers, locked in by tabs.
  • Springs and screws under each center set the tension so layers turn smoothly.
